# Content Structure

## Personal Information
Name, role, introduction, and relevant personal information.

## Hero
Short introduction and primary call-to-action.

## About
Personal background, interests, approach, and story.

## Skills
Relevant technical skills and technologies.

## Projects
Selected projects with:
- Project name
- Description
- Technologies
- Key highlights
- Links where applicable

## Experience
Relevant professional or personal experience.

## Education
Relevant educational information, if applicable.

## Contact
Preferred contact methods and relevant links.

## Content Rules
- Use the content provided by the user.
- Do not fabricate information.
- Do not add fake testimonials, statistics, clients, companies, or achievements.
- Keep copy clear and authentic.

## Source
Detailed content will be provided in the main agent prompt.
